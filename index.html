Youth Net and Counselling-YONECO

Youth Net and Counselling (YONECO) is a Malawian non-governmental organisation established in 1997 to address social and developmental challenges affecting young people, women and children. The organisation focuses on youth empowerment, child protection, gender equality, sexual and reproductive health and rights (SRHR), human rights, good governance and community development. YONECO implements various programmes aimed at promoting access to information, protecting vulnerable populations, preventing gender-based violence (GBV) and strengthening community participation in development. It also operates nationwide communication and support services, including YONECO FM Radio and the Tithandizane Helpline, which provides support through toll-free lines for children, young people, survivors of GBV and people affected by drug and substance abuse. Through its rights-based and community-centred approach, YONECO contributes to building a healthy, empowered, resilient and inclusive Malawian society.
